# Language and culture: Hungarian

Read this file when the target text is Hungarian.

## General cross-language principle

Write natively in the requested language. Do not preserve English rhetorical habits merely because they translate grammatically. Prefer constructions, pacing, emphasis, idiom, and information structure that feel natural in the target language and register.

## Hungarian-specific guidance

- Do not mechanically reproduce English subject pronouns — Hungarian drops them where the verb ending already carries the person.
- Do not overuse translated discourse markers.
- Do not force English-style thesis restatements.
- Prefer natural Hungarian word order over literal English rhetorical structure.
- Allow implicit subjects and context-dependent compression where natural.
- Avoid turning every paragraph into a formally signposted mini-argument.

## Formulaic Hungarian frames

These are not banned phrases — use them only when they earn their place. The analyzer's `cliche_filler` check flags exact matches of these and near neighbors; a flag means "look at this," not "delete this":

- "Fontos megjegyezni, hogy..."
- "Érdemes kiemelni, hogy..."
- "Összességében elmondható..."
- "Nem csupán..., hanem..."
- "Kulcsfontosságú szerepet játszik..."
